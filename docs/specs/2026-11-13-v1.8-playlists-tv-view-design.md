# TV Photos 1.8: playlists and the page on the TV

Approved in chat on 2026-11-13. Builds on 1.7.1 (versionCode 9).

## Goals

1. **Playlists.** Named sets of photos and combinations (e.g. Italy, Japan). An item can be in several
   playlists; nothing is copied.
2. **What plays.** Each playlist has a *Play on the TV* switch. The screensaver plays the union of the
   switched-on playlists; with none on it plays every visible photo and combination (1.7 behaviour). If
   the switched-on playlists have nothing visible, it falls back to everything visible.
3. **The page on the TV.** The TV Photos app opens the same phone page in a WebView, in a TV layout, signed
   in automatically, driven with the remote's arrows, OK and Back. Everything works there except adding
   photos, framing and combining, which say "Do this on your phone".

Hidden items stay hidden inside playlists. Deleting a photo or combination removes it from every playlist
(a combination deleted because its photo was deleted goes too). Deleting a playlist keeps its items.

## Data (library.json)

```json
{"photos": {...}, "combos": [...],
 "playlists": [{"id": "p<13 digits>_<6 hex>", "name": "Italy", "on": true,
                "items": ["<photo>.jpg", "c<13>_<6>"]}]}
```

- Name: trimmed, 1-40 characters, no control characters. Duplicate names are allowed.
- Items keep their order of adding; duplicates are ignored. Items that no longer exist are dropped when
  the page lists them and when the screensaver builds its round.

## Server API (behind the PIN cookie)

| Route | Body | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| `GET /api/photos` | | adds `playlists` (existing items only) |
| `POST /api/playlist` | `name` (+ `id` to rename) | creates (returns `id`) or renames |
| `POST /api/playlist/delete` | `id` | deletes the playlist only |
| `POST /api/playlist/items` | `id`, `add=1/0`, `names`, `combos` | adds or removes items |
| `POST /api/playlist/play` | `id`, `on=1/0` | the *Play on the TV* switch |
| `POST /api/upload?playlist=id` | photo | as before, then adds the new photo to that playlist |

## Phone page

- Chip row under the header: **All**, then each playlist with its item count, then **+ New** (prompt for
  a name). A small ▶ mark on the chips that play. The chosen chip filters both grids.
- In a playlist: a bar with *Play on the TV* switch, **Rename**, **Delete playlist**. **+ Add** uploads into
  it. Select mode shows **Remove** (from the playlist) besides Combine, Hide/Show, Delete.
- In All: Select mode shows **Add to…**, which lists the playlists (and *New playlist…*).
- Header count unchanged; a line under the chips says what plays: "Playing: Italy, Japan" or
  "Playing: all photos".

## TV view

- `ManageActivity` (new): a full-screen WebView on `http://127.0.0.1:8080/?tv=1`. Before loading, it sets
  the `tvp` cookie from `Store.token()` for 127.0.0.1. JS on, no file access, navigation to any other
  origin blocked. A `WebChromeClient` so `confirm()`/`prompt()` show as TV dialogs.
- Back: asks the page (`window.tvBack()`); if the page has nothing to close, the activity finishes.
- MainActivity gets a **Photos & playlists** button (first, focused by default).
- The page with `?tv=1`: `body.tv` class, larger type and tiles (5 columns), visible focus ring, no
  `+ Add`, framing and combining buttons replaced by the hint "Do this on your phone". A small spatial
  navigation script moves focus with the arrows to the nearest focusable element in that direction and
  scrolls it into view. Opening a photo on the TV opens a simple sheet: Show on TV, Add to playlist /
  Remove from this playlist, Delete.

## Screensaver

`nextSlide()` builds the round as in 1.7, then, when any playlist is on, keeps only items in the union of
the switched-on playlists (falling back to all if that leaves nothing). `refresh()` stays as in 1.7.
